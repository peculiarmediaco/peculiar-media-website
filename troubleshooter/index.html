What's wrong with the vocal?

VOCALS / LEVEL

Is the whole vocal too loud or too quiet, or does the level keep changing?

TRY THIS FIRST

Adjust the vocal level.

If the vocal is consistently too loud or too quiet, start with the simplest fix: change the fader level.

Don't reach for compression just because the vocal is sitting at the wrong overall level.

Subtractive thought: Before turning the vocal up, ask whether something else could come down. A competing guitar, keyboard, cymbal, or other element may be masking it.

TRY THIS FIRST

Try volume automation.

If entire lines or sections are too loud or too quiet, automation lets you correct the level deliberately instead of asking a compressor to solve every change.

Bring down sections that jump forward. Bring up sections that disappear. Listen again before adding anything else.

Then consider compression: Compression can help control shorter level changes and make the performance feel more consistent, but it doesn't replace thoughtful level balance.

TRY THIS FIRST

Listen for individual problem words.

If a few words suddenly jump forward or disappear, you may not need to process the entire vocal more heavily.

Try clip gain or volume automation on the specific problem areas first.

If it keeps happening: Gentle compression may help reduce the difference between louder and quieter moments.
